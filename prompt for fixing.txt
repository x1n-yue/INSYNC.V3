5. Never claim something is secure/fixed from "build passes." Every fix needs a test or a documented verification step. Be honest about what could not be verified (live Supabase, browser, a11y).
6. Use disposable/synthetic data only. No real credentials, no `.env` secrets in output.
7. Do not run `npm audit fix --force`. Dependency changes must be tested and compatible with Node/plugins.
8.
